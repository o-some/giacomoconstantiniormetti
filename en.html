05 / GOOD TO KNOW

YOUR QUESTIONS.
CLEAR ANSWERS.

Can I discuss nutrition and meal prep?

Yes. You can choose eating routines, shopping plans and meal preparation as a focus for your conversation. We agree on the specific scope beforehand.

How much does one-to-one coaching cost?

One-to-one fitness coaching costs €100 per hour (60 minutes). We agree on the time, location and specific content before booking.

Why choose coaching rather than training alone?

One-to-one coaching gives you space to ask questions and receive personal feedback. This can help you approach training more mindfully and see your next steps more clearly.

Do I need previous training experience?

Your training so far is a starting point for our conversation. Simply share where you are and what you would like. That helps us work out which guidance suits you.

How do I know which coaching suits me?

Start with your goal: more strength, more movement or more consistent routines. In a personal conversation, we turn that into concrete next steps.

How much time should I allow?

That depends on your goal and your everyday life. The time you have available is part of the plan from the start.

What should I think about before our first conversation?

What would you like to change? What does your training look like now? What schedule would be realistic? Three honest answers are a good place to start.
